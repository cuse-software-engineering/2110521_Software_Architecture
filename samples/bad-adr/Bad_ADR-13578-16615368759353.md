## ADR 1: Confirmation for creating reviews hub

**Title**
ADR 1: Confirmation for creating reviews hub

**Context**
We want to know if we can create a hub for CU subject and instructor reviews to solve the issue where reviews were sparse.

**Decision**
- We will interview people for their opinions about online reviews.
- We will decide which tools will be used to create a platform.
- We will learn about microservice architecture.

**Status**
Proposed

**Consequences**

*Positives*
- 8 out of 10 interviewees agreed that creating a hub for course review will solve this issue.
- We made an appointment for a future meeting about tools and architecture used for this software.

*Negatives*
- We may have a problem with defamation law, since students might criticize the instructor and unjustly harm their reputation.
